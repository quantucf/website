In collaboration with AmStat, we will use approximately 15 publicly available market and macroeconomic predictors to forecast the next month’s U.S. equity market return. We will compare regression models with two machine-learning models on held-out periods, assess forecast accuracy across market conditions, and evaluate the performance of a forecast-based trading rule net of transaction costs.
